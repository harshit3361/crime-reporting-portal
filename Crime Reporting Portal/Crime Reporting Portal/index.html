    PROCESS

    How the portal works

    From complaint to resolution in four transparent steps.

    1

    File a report

    Fill in the secure form with the incident category, location and statement.

    2

    Get a Reference ID

    A unique CR-ID is generated instantly so you can follow your case.

    3

    Officer review

    The case is assigned to the right division and investigated.

    4

    Track & resolve

    Check live status updates until the case is marked resolved.

      ABOUT US

      About CrimePortal

      This platform connects citizens with national emergency units to enable rapid response, whistleblower anonymity, and digital tracking of civil security reports.

      🎯

      Our Mission

      To make reporting a crime as simple as sending a message, while guaranteeing that every complaint is recorded, routed to the right unit and followed to closure.

      👁️

      Our Vision

      A transparent, citizen-first policing ecosystem where no incident goes unreported out of fear, confusion or inconvenience.

      🤝

      Our Values

      Confidentiality, accountability, speed and respect for every person who comes forward.

      Portal at a glance

      ModuleWhat it does
      Report IncidentValidated complaint form with anonymous option; generates a unique Reference ID.
      Track CaseLooks up a Reference ID and shows its stage timeline and history.
      Live MapLeaflet map with filterable, searchable incident pins and list.
      Safety HubSearchable tips, saved checklist and a scam risk calculator.
      Officer DashboardCaptcha-protected login; officers update status, filter and export reports.

      Technology used

      HTML5CSS3 (Flexbox & Grid)JavaScript (ES6)Leaflet.js mapsLocalStorageResponsive design

      Academic project: incidents on the map are sample data. Reports you file are stored only in your own browser (LocalStorage) so the tracking and officer features can be demonstrated without a server.
